// The Glory of the Śrī Chaitanya Sāraswat Maṭh (Śrīla B.R. Śrīdhar Mahārāja). Editorial glosses.

# verse-1
śrīmach-chaitanya-sārasvata-maṭhavara-udgīta-kīrtir = the best temple, Śrī Chaitanya Sārasvata Maṭh, whose fame is sung
jaya-śrīṁ = the fortune of victory

# verse-2
bibhrat = bearing
sambhāti = shines
gaṅgā-taṭa-nikaṭa-navadvīpa-kolādri-rāje = at Koladvīpa, the king of hills in Navadvīpa, near the Ganges bank

# verse-3
yatra = where
śrī-gaura-sārasvata-mata-niratā-gaura-gāthā = those devoted to Śrī Gaura-Sarasvatī’s teaching, Gaura’s glories
gṛṇanti = sing

# verse-4
nityaṁ = always
śrī-kṛtamati-guru-gaurāṅga-rādhā-jitāśā = whose hearts are set on Śrī Guru, Gaurāṅga and Rādhā-(Kṛṣṇa)
