@ sri-siksastakam-1-110
// Śrī Śikṣāṣṭakam 1 (Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura, Gītāvalī). Editorial glosses.

# verse-1
pīta-varaṇa = golden-complexioned
kali-pāvana = the purifier of Kali
gāoyāi = sings
aichana = thus
bhāva-bibhorā = overwhelmed with ecstasy

# verse-2
chitta-darpaṇa-parimārjana-kārī = which cleanses the mirror of the heart
chitta-bihārī = which delights the heart

# verse-3
helā-bhava-dāva-nirvāpaṇa-vṛtti = which easily extinguishes the forest fire of material existence
kleśa-nivṛtti = which removes all distress

# verse-4
śreyaḥ-kumuda-bidhu-jyotsnā-prakāśa = the moonlight that opens the lotus of good fortune
bhakti-vilāsa = the play of devotion

# verse-5
viśuddha-vidyā-vadhu = the bride of pure knowledge
siddha-svarūpa = one’s perfect identity

# verse-6
ānanda-payo-nidhi-vardhana-kīrti = which increases the ocean of bliss
plābana-mūrti = the embodiment of a flood

# verse-7
pījuṣa-svāda-pradātā = which gives the taste of nectar
prema-bidhātā = which bestows divine love

# verse-8
bhakativinoda-svātma-snapana-vidhān = which bathes Bhaktivinoda’s soul
prema-nidān = the storehouse of love
