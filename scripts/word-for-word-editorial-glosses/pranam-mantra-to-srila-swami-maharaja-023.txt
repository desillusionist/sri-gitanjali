gurvajñaṁ = his guru’s order
śīrasi-dhṛtvā = holding on his head
śaktyāveśa = empowered
sva-rūpine = in his own form
hare-kṛṣṇeti = as “Hare Kṛṣṇa”
mantreṇa = by the mantra
pāśchatya-prāchya-tāriṇe = who delivered West and East
viśvāchārya = among the world āchāryas
prabaryāya = the foremost
pradāyine = the giver
gaura-śrī-rūpa-siddhānta-sarasvatī = of the conclusions of Gaura, Śrī Rūpa and Sarasvatī
niṣevine = the servant
rādhā-kṛṣṇa-padāmbhoja-bhṛṅgāya = the bee at Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet
gurave = to the guru
