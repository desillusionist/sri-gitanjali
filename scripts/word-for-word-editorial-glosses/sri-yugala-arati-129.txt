// Śrī Yugala-ārati (Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura, Gītāvalī). Editorial glosses.

# verse-1
yugala-milan = the meeting of the Couple
karaye = perform
sakhī-goṇ = the sakhīs

# verse-2
tri-bhaṅga-sundara = beautiful, bending in three places
pītāmbara = in yellow cloth
śikhi-pucha-chūḍā-manohara = charming with a peacock-feather crown

# verse-3
lalita-mādhava-bāme = to the left of charming Mādhava
vṛṣabhānu-kanyā = Vṛṣabhānu’s daughter
nīla-vasanā = in blue garments
gaurī = fair
rūpe = in beauty
guṇe = and qualities
dhanyā = glorious

# verse-4
alaṅkāra = ornaments
hari-mano-vimohana = enchanting Hari’s mind

# verse-5
viśākhādi = Viśākhā and others
sakhī-goṇ = the sakhīs
rāge = melodies
priya-narma-sakhī = the dear playful friends
chāmāra = chāmaras

# verse-6
śrī-rādhā-mādhava-pada-sarasija-āśe = hoping for the lotus feet of Śrī Rādhā-Mādhava
sakhī-pade = at the sakhīs’ feet
bhāse = floats
