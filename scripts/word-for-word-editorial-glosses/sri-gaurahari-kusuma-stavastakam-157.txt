@ sri-gaurahari-kusuma-stavastakam-157
// Śrī Gaurahari-kusuma-stavāṣṭakam (Śrīla B.R. Śrīdhar Mahārāja). Editorial glosses.

# *
gaurahariṁ = Gaurahari
gauraharim = Gaurahari

# verse-1
guru-rūpa-virājita-nanda-sutaṁ = Nanda’s son, shining in the form of the guru
sutanūnnata-kāñchana-bhūmi-dharam = beautiful, exalted, bearing a golden form
dharaṇī-jana-tāraṇa-tuṅga-tariṁ = the great boat to deliver the people of the earth

# verse-2
vṛṣabhānu-sutā-dyuti-bhāva-vṛtaṁ = covered by the lustre and mood of Vṛṣabhānu’s daughter
vṛta-chitta-nijādbhuta-rūpa-madhum = His heart absorbed in the sweetness of His own wonderful form
madhu-vibhrama-nāmada-śaśva-dariṁ = sweetly intoxicated, ever giving the Name

# verse-3
uditākhila-gaurava-gauḍapuraṁ = who revealed the full glory of Gauḍa’s abode
puraṭojjvala-maṅgala-mūrta-rasam = rasa embodied in a radiant golden, auspicious form
rasanotsava-kīrtana-kṛṣṇa-hariṁ = chanting “Kṛṣṇa, Hari”, a festival for the tongue

# verse-4
nija-pārṣada-darśana-datta-śubhaṁ = who gave the auspicious sight of His associates
śubha-nāma-sudhā-kṛta-matta-janam = who maddened the people with the nectar of the auspicious Name
janatāgha-hara-sthita-chitta-darim = intent on removing the people’s sins

# verse-5
dadataṁ = giving
satataṁ = always
nija-nāma-dhanaṁ = the wealth of His own Name
vai = indeed
vanitāṁ = women
vitaran = distributing
vraja-bhaktim-adatta-charīṁ = devotion of Vraja never given before

# verse-6
patitādhama-dīna-dayādra-hṛdaṁ = whose heart melts with compassion for the fallen and lowly
hṛdayāśrita-yāchaka-veśa-kṛtam = who took the dress of a beggar from that feeling
kṛta-veṣa-yati-śrita-nīla-giriṁ = who, as a sannyāsī, took shelter at Nīlāchala

# verse-7
śruti-kīrtita-pūruṣa-rukṣma-ruchiṁ = the golden Person praised by the Vedas
ruchi-rāga-niṣevaṇa-dāna-param = intent on giving loving service in spontaneous attraction
paramārtha-purāṇa-vigīta-hariṁ = Hari, sung in the Purāṇas as the supreme goal

# verse-8
bahu-nartana-kīrtana-matta-karam = maddened by much dancing and chanting
karatāla-mṛdaṅga-vibhaṅga-param = amid the rhythms of karatālas and mṛdaṅgas
paramādara-pāmara-śāntipurīṁ = most dear to the fallen, the abode of peace
