@ srita-kamala-135
// Śrita Kamalā (Śrīla Jayadeva Gosvāmī, Gīta-govinda). Editorial glosses.
// Note: the app text has “gaṣjana” and “raṣjana” in verse 3, probably typos for gañjana and rañjana; glossed as written.

# verse-1
śrita-kamalā-kuchha-maṇḍala = embraced by the bosom of Kamalā (Lakṣmī)
dhṛta-kuṇḍala = wearing earrings
kalita-lalita-vana-māla = adorned with a charming forest garland

# verse-2
dina-maṇi-maṇḍala-maṇḍana = shining like the orb of the sun
bhava-khaṇḍana = destroyer of material existence
muni-jana-mānasa-haṁsa = the swan in the lake of the sages’ minds

# verse-3
kāliya-viṣa-dhara-gaṣjana = subduer of the venomous Kāliya
jana-raṣjana = delighter of the people
yadukula-nalina-dineśa = the sun for the lotus of the Yadu dynasty

# verse-4
madhu-mura-naraka-vināśana = destroyer of Madhu, Mura and Naraka
garuḍāsana = seated on Garuḍa
sura-kula-keli-nidāna = the source of the gods’ joy

# verse-5
amala-kamala-dala-lochana = with eyes like spotless lotus petals
bhava-mochana = deliverer from material existence
tribhuvana-bhuvana-nidhāna = the abode of the three worlds

# verse-6
janaka-sutā-kṛta-bhūṣaṇa = adorned by Janaka’s daughter (Sītā)
jita-dūṣaṇa = conqueror of Dūṣaṇa
samara-śamita-daśa-kaṇṭha = who killed ten-headed Rāvaṇa in battle

# verse-7
abhinava-jala-dhara-sundara = beautiful like a fresh rain cloud
dhṛta-mandara = holder of Mandara (and Govardhana)
śrī-mukha-chandra-chakora = the chakora bird for the moon of Śrī (Rādhā)’s face

# verse-8
charaṇaṁ = at the feet
praṇatā = bowed
vayam = we
bhāvaya = consider
kuru = please give
kuśalaṁ = welfare
praṇateṣu = to those who bow

# verse-9
śrī-jayadeva-kaver = of the poet Śrī Jayadeva
idaṁ = this
kurute = gives
mudam = joy
maṅgalam = auspicious
ujjvala-gītaṁ = brilliant song
