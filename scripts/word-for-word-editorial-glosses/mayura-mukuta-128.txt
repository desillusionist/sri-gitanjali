// Mayūra mukuṭa (Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura). Editorial glosses.

# *

govardhana-dhārī = the lifter of Govardhana

# verse-1
mayura-mukuṭa = with a peacock-feather crown
pītāmbara-dhārī = wearing yellow cloth

# verse-2
kuñja-bihārī = who sports in the groves

# verse-4
gopī-jana-ballabha = the beloved of the gopīs
vaṁśī-bihārī = who plays with the flute
