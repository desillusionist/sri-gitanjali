// Ki-rūpe pāibo sevā (Śrīla Narottama Dāsa Ṭhākura, Prārthanā). Editorial glosses.

# verse-1
ki-rūpe = how
pāibo = shall I attain
durāchāra = misbehaved
śrī-guru-vaiṣṇave = for Śrī Guru and the Vaiṣṇavas

# verse-2
māyāte = in illusion
magana = absorbed
vaiṣṇavete = for the Vaiṣṇavas
leśa-mātra = even a trace

# verse-3
viṣoye = in sense objects
bhuliya = forgetting
andha = blind
hoinu = I became
gole = on the neck
phā̐sa = a noose
dite = to put
phere = wanders
piśāchī = the witch

# verse-4
māyāre = Māyā
koriyā = making
chāḍāna = release
jāya = is possible
sādhu-kṛpā = the mercy of the saints
āro = other
nahiko = there is not
upāya = means

# verse-5
adoṣa = faultless
daraśi-prabhu = the Lord who sees (no faults)
patita-uddhāra = the deliverer of the fallen
karaho = please make
nistāra = deliverance
