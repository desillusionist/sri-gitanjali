@ sri-dayita-das-pranati-panchakam-138
// Śrī Dayita-dāsa-praṇati-pañcakam (Śrīla B.R. Śrīdhar Mahārāja). Editorial glosses, read with the translation.

# *
śubhadodaya-divase = on the auspicious day of his appearance
vṛṣaravijā-nija-dayitam = to the beloved of the daughter of Vṛṣabhānu
charaṇāntika-parichāraka-sahitam = with the servants at his feet

# verse-1
bhayabhañjana = dispelling fear
jayaśaṁsana-karuṇāyata-nayanam = whose large merciful eyes proclaim victory
kanakotpala = the golden lotus
janakojjvala-rasasāgara-chayanam = born from the brilliant ocean of rasa
mukharīkṛta-dharaṇītala-harikīrtana-rasanam = whose tongue fills the earth with Hari-kīrtana
kṣitipāvana-bhavatāraṇa-pihitāruṇa-vasanam = robed in saffron that purifies the earth and delivers from material existence

# verse-2
śaraṇāgata-bhajanavrata-chirapālana-charaṇam = whose feet eternally protect the surrendered devotees
sukṛtālaya-saralāśaya-sujanākhila-varaṇam = worshipped by all fortunate, sincere and good souls
harisādhana-kṛtabādhana = those who obstruct Hari’s service
janaśāsana-kalanam = accepting to correct the people
sacharāchara-karuṇākara-nikhilāśiva-dalanam = merciful to all beings, crushing all inauspiciousness

# verse-3
atilaukika-gatitaulika-ratikautuka-vapuṣam = whose form moves with a joyful charm beyond the world
atidaivata-mativaiṣṇava-yati-vaibhava-puruṣam = of more than godly intellect, the glory of Vaiṣṇava sannyāsīs
sasanātana-raghurūpaka-paramāṇugacharitam = whose life closely follows Sanātana, Raghunātha and Rūpa
suvichāraka = a perfect thinker
jīvaka = Jīva (Gosvāmī)
sādhubhiruditam = described by the saints

# verse-4
sarasītaṭa-sukhadoṭaja-nikaṭapriyabhajanam = serving his Beloved near the happy hut on the bank of the lake (Rādhā Kuṇḍa)
lalitāmukha-lalanākula-paramādarayajanam = most dear to the damsels headed by Lalitā
vrajakānana-bahumānana-kamalapriyanayanam = the favourite of Kamala (mañjarī), honoured in Vraja’s forests
guṇamañjari-garimā-guṇa-harivāsanavayanam = building Hari’s residence with the qualities of Guṇamañjarī

# verse-5
vimalotsavam = immaculate joy (the joy of Vimalā Devī)
amalotkala-puruṣottama-jananam = born at Puruṣottama in pure Orissa
patitoddhṛti-karuṇāstṛti-kṛtanūtana-pulinam = revealing at Navadvīpa his mercy to deliver the fallen
mathurāpura-puruṣottama-samagaurapuraṭanam = circumambulating Gaura’s abode like Mathurā and Puruṣottama
harikāmaka-haridhāmaka-harināmaka-raṭanam = always proclaiming Hari’s desire, abode and Name
