// Gaṅgādhara Sadāśiva Praṇām-mantram (Śrīla B.S. Govinda Mahārāja). Editorial glosses.

# verse-1
devādidevam-ahibhūṣaṇam-indukāśaṁ = the first of the gods, adorned with snakes, shining like the moon
paṅchānanaṁ = five-faced
paśupatiṁ = the Lord of beings (Paśupati)
varadaṁ = the giver of boons
prasannam = gracious
gaṅgādharaṁ = the bearer of the Ganges
praṇatapālakamāśutoṣaṁ = the protector of the surrendered, quickly pleased
sadāśiva-haripriya-chandramaulim = Sadāśiva, dear to Hari, crowned with the moon
