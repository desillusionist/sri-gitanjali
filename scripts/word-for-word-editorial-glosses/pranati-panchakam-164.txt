@ pranati-panchakam-164
// Praṇati-pañcakam (Śrīla B.S. Govinda Mahārāja). Editorial glosses.

# verse-1
nava-nalina-gañjitaṁ = shaming fresh lotuses
kusuma-dala-maṇḍitaṁ = adorned with flower petals
pāda-yugam-abhaya-varadānam = whose two feet give fearlessness and blessings
kanaka-ghana-ninditaṁ = shaming solid gold
vapura-mara-vanditaṁ = whose form is honoured by the gods
naṭana-gati-gaurava-nidānam = the source of the glory of dancing movement

# verse-2
bhaje = I worship
śrīdhara-svāminam-aniśam = Śrīdhara Svāmī always
śarada-vidhu-nindi-mukha-padma-mati-śobhanaṁ = whose lotus face, shaming the autumn moon, is most beautiful
gopī-tilaka-charchita-subhālam = whose fine forehead is marked with gopīchandana tilaka

# verse-3
gamana-jita-vāraṇaṁ = whose gait surpasses an elephant’s
śamana-bhaya-tāraṇaṁ = who delivers from the fear of death
prakṛti-ruchi-nindi-kṛta-veśam = whose dress shames worldly taste
prakṛti-ruchi-nambhrama-suveśam = whose fine dress is free from worldly taste
phulla-kara-pallavaṁ = with blossoming, budlike hands
bhakta-jana-vallabhaṁ = dear to the devotees
tuṅga-pṛthulaṁ = high and broad
hṛdaya-deśam = chest

# verse-4
gaura-puraṭa-saurabhaṁ = with the fragrance of Gaura’s golden abode
kṛṣṇa-puraṭa-gauravaṁ = with the glory of Kṛṣṇa’s abode
vibudha-kula-padma-naliśam = the lord of the lotus of the learned
bhakta-kula-daṇḍanaṁ = chastiser (of offenders) for the devotees
bhaṇḍa-mata-khaṇḍanaṁ = refuter of deceitful doctrines
bhakta-kula-nandana-yatīśam = the lord of sannyāsīs who delights the devotees
