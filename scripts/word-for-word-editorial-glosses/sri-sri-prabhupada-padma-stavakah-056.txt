@ sri-sri-prabhupada-padma-stavakah-056
// Śrī Śrī Prabhupāda-padma-stavakaḥ (Śrīla B.R. Śrīdhar Mahārāja). Editorial glosses.

# *
prabhupāda-padam = unto the feet of Śrīla Prabhupāda

# verse-1
sujanārvuda-rādhita-pāda-yugaṁ = whose two feet are served by millions of saintly souls
yuga-dharma-dhurandhara-pātra-varam = the best of those who uphold the religion of the age
varadābhaya-dāyaka-pūjya-padaṁ = whose worshipable feet give blessings and fearlessness

# verse-2
bhajanorjita = rich in internal worship
sajjana-saṅgha-patiṁ = the leader of the community of saints
patitādhika-kāruṇikaika-gatim = most merciful to the fallen, their only shelter
gati-vañchita-vañchakāchintya-padaṁ = whose inconceivable feet are the shelter even for the deceived and deceivers

# verse-3
atikomala-kāñchana-dīrgha-tanuṁ = with a very tender, tall, golden form
tanu-nindita-hema-mṛṇāla-madam = whose body shames the pride of golden lotus stems
madanārvuda-vandita-chandra-padaṁ = whose moonlike toenails are worshipped by millions of Cupids

# verse-4
nija-sevaka-tāraka-rañji-vidhuṁ = the moon delighting the stars, his own servants
vidhutāhita-huṅkṛta-siṁha-varam = the best of lions, whose roar drives away the malicious
varaṇāgata-bāliśa-śanda-padaṁ = whose feet give good fortune to the ignorant who take shelter

# verse-5
vipulīkṛta-vaibhava-gaura-bhuvaṁ = who revealed the vast splendour of Gaura’s land
bhuvaneṣu = throughout the worlds
vikīrtita-gaura-dayam = who proclaimed Gaura’s mercy
dayanīya-gaṇārpita-gaura-padaṁ = who gave Gaura’s feet to the deserving

# verse-6
chira-gaura-janāśraya-viśva-guruṁ = the universal guru, eternal shelter of Gaura’s devotees
guru-gaura-kiśoraka-dāsya-param = absorbed in serving his guru, Gaura Kiśora
paramādṛta-bhaktivinoda-padaṁ = who supremely honours Bhaktivinoda

# verse-7
raghu-rūpa-sanātana-kīrti-dharaṁ = the bearer of the fame of Raghunātha, Rūpa and Sanātana
dharaṇī-tala-kīrtita-jīva-kavim = sung on earth as the poet Jīva
kavirāja-narottama-sakhya-padaṁ = the friend of Kavirāja and Narottama

# verse-8
mūrti-dharaṁ = in person
dharaṇī-bhara-hāraka-gaura-janam = Gaura’s devotee who removes the earth’s burden
janakādhika-vatsala-snigdha-padaṁ = more affectionate than a father

# verse-9
śaraṇāgata-kiṅkara-kalpa-taruṁ = a desire tree for his surrendered servants
taru-dhik-kṛta = shaming a tree
dhīra = in forbearance
vadānya = and magnanimity
varadendra-gaṇārchita-divya-padaṁ = whose divine feet are worshipped by the great givers of boons

# verse-10
parahaṁsa-varaṁ = the best of paramahaṁsas
paramārtha-patiṁ = the lord of the supreme goal
patitodharaṇe = to deliver the fallen
kṛta-veśa-yatim = who took the dress of a sannyāsī
yati-rāja-gaṇaiḥ = by the kings of sannyāsīs
parisevya-padaṁ = whose feet are served

# verse-11
vṛṣabhānu-sutā-dayitānucharaṁ = the dear follower of Vṛṣabhānu’s daughter
charaṇāśrita-reṇu-dharas = bearing the dust of his sheltering feet
mahad-adbhuta-pāvana-śakti-padaṁ = whose feet have great and wonderful purifying power
