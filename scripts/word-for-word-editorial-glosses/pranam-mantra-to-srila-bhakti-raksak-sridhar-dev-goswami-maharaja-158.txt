@ pranam-mantra-to-srila-bhakti-raksak-sridhar-dev-goswami-maharaja-158
// Praṇām Mantra to Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣak Śrīdhar Dev-Goswāmī Mahārāja (Śrīla B.S. Govinda Mahārāja). Editorial glosses.

# *
śrīdharaṁ = unto Śrīdhara
bhakti-rakṣakaṁ = Bhakti Rakṣaka
jagad-gurum = the guru of the world

# verse-1
śrī-svarūpa-rāya-rūpa = of Śrī Svarūpa, Rāya and Rūpa
jīva-bhāva-sambharaṁ = who carries the mood, and Jīva’s
varṇa-dharma-nirviśeṣa-sarvaloka-nistaram = the deliverer of all people without regard to caste or creed
śrī-sarasvatī-priyañ = dear to Śrī Sarasvatī Ṭhākura
bhakti-sundarāśrayaṁ = the shelter of Bhakti Sundara (Govinda Mahārāja)

# verse-2
sindhu-chandra-parvatendu-śāka-janma-līlanam = whose birth pastime was in Śaka 1817 (seven, one, eight, one)
śuddha-dīpta-rāga-bhakti-gauravānuśīlanam = who cultivated the glory of pure, radiant spontaneous devotion
bindu-chandra-ratna-soma-śāka-lochanāntaram = who disappeared from sight in Śaka 1910 (zero, one, nine, one)

# verse-3
suruchi = beautiful lustre
rāngaṁ = of colour
sundaraṁ = beautiful
saumya-mūrtiṁ = of gentle form
vibudha-kula-vareṇyaṁ = honoured by the learned
śrī-guruṁ = Śrī Guru
siddhi-pūrtim = the fulfilment of perfection
taruṇa-tapana-vāsaṁ = robed like the rising sun
bhaktidañ = giving devotion
chid-vilāsaṁ = of spiritual pastimes
mano = O mind
śambidhānam = the bestower of good
