@ sriman-nityananda-dvadasakam-156::verse-1

@ sriman-nityananda-dvadasakam-156::verse-2

@ sriman-nityananda-dvadasakam-156::verse-3

@ sriman-nityananda-dvadasakam-156::verse-4
